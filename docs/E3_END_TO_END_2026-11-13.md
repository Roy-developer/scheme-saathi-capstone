## Requirements to finish E3

1. **Commit the missing E3 docs** (above) into the repo.
2. **Decide the IGNDPS lesson.** Its dev split is `null`, so it can't be used as a source until the split is assigned. Its package `reasons.eligible` text also needs fixing.
3. **Build playbook 1.1.0** with `scripts/build_freeze_manifest.py`, using only the approved lessons. Do not hand-edit the manifest.
4. **Held-out check for 1.1.0.** A paid 1.1.0 run on the check split is in-sample. A clean claim needs unseen cases: at least 40 total, two independent human labelers, and three frozen repeats (see `eval/held_out/README.md`). Current held-out status: 16 shared cases on IGNWPS-MH and IGNOAPS-MH, person-level only, not enough for the 1.1.0 claim.
5. **Budget and go-ahead.** The $20 cap is recorded in the manifest. A paid run needs an explicit go-ahead at the time it runs.
6. **Record the result.** Report as directional unless step 4 is met. Keep the results summary in `eval/reports/` and link it here.
